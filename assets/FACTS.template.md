# Cover Fact Ledger

Update this file before rendering. Every factual statement visible on a cover and in the final `COVER_PROMPT.md` must have one row. Editorial framing and interface illustrations must also be labeled so they cannot be mistaken for verified facts.

## Publication gate

- [ ] No sample fact or placeholder remains on a deliverable.
- [ ] Every version, date, parameter, price, ranking, license, and release-status claim has a current first-party source.
- [ ] Every user-experience claim was supplied or confirmed by the user.
- [ ] Every interface mockup is labeled as an illustration, or replaced with a real sourced screenshot.
- [ ] Brand marks and downloaded assets have been checked against their current usage terms in `assets/SOURCES.md`.

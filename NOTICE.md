# Notices and asset provenance

## Independent implementation and visual inspiration

The content-routing model, cover-prompt workflow, portrait rules, current HTML/CSS templates, and rendering scripts are implemented for this project. The public `assets/templates/wechat.html` was rewritten from a blank implementation with its own structure, design tokens, class names, and export nodes.

Early visual research was inspired by the Swiss social-card idea in [`op7418/guizang-social-card-skill`](https://github.com/op7418/guizang-social-card-skill), authored by `op7418`. The current template does not use that project's code scaffold. This acknowledgment records design inspiration; it does not claim that this repository or its current template originates from that project.

This repository independently chooses GNU AGPL-3.0 for its code, documentation, and templates. The license does not relicense the portraits, third-party trademarks, organization avatars, screenshots, or other externally supplied media described below.

## Brand assets

The files under `assets/brand/` were fetched from official GitHub organization avatars for Qwen, DeepSeek, OpenAI, Moonshot AI, and xAI. Their URLs and fetch date are recorded in `assets/SOURCES.md`.

These files are bundled in public examples as product-identification references and routing placeholders. Inclusion does not imply endorsement, affiliation, or permission to reuse a company's trademark in another campaign. Before publishing a new cover, check the current official brand resources and terms or replace the asset.

## Portrait assets

The files under `assets/portrait/` were supplied by the repository owner from their own event album. The subject has explicitly authorized their public display in this repository and its example covers. No expiring signed URL is retained, and the bundled JPEG files contain no detected EXIF/GPS profile.

That display authorization is not a general identity or stock-photo license. Do not reuse these portraits as another person's identity or in an unrelated campaign without the subject's separate permission. Public forks should replace them with assets they are authorized to publish.
